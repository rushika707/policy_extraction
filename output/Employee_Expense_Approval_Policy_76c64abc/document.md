## Employee Expense Approval Policy

Policy ID:

FIN-EXP-2026-004

Version:

1.0

Effective Date:

15 September 2026

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for reviewing and approving employee expenses. Expenses must be supported by a valid business purpose and the required approval before reimbursement.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to employees, contractors and temporary staff submitting business-related expenses.

## 3. Expense Approval Matrix

| Expense category   | Amount       | Required approval   | Outcome if approval missing   |
|--------------------|--------------|---------------------|-------------------------------|
| Travel             | Up to £500   | Line manager        | REJECT                        |
| Travel             | Above £500   | Department head     | REJECT                        |
| Equipment          | Up to £1,000 | Line manager        | REJECT                        |
| Equipment          | Above £1,000 | Department head     | REJECT                        |
| Training           | Up to £2,000 | Line manager        | REJECT                        |
| Training           | Above £2,000 | Department head     | REJECT                        |

## 4. Mandatory Requirements

ER-01 - Every expense must contain an employee identifier, expense category, amount and business purpose.

ER-02 - An expense must have the approval required by the Expense Approval Matrix before reimbursement.

ER-03 - An expense with a non-positive amount is invalid and must be rejected.

ER-04 - Expenses in categories not defined by this policy require Finance review before reimbursement.

ER-05 - An expense must not be reimbursed when the required approval is missing.

## 5. Exceptions

An exception may be granted only when it is documented, includes a business justification, identifies an accountable approver and has an expiry date. An exception without an expiry date is invalid.

## 6. Decision Outcomes

The policy does not provide enough information for an automatic decision or the category is undefined.

| Decision   | Meaning                                                                         |
|------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| APPROVE    | All applicable requirements are satisfied and the required approval is present. |
| REJECT     | A mandatory requirement is violated or required approval is missing.            |
| REVIEW     | The policy does not provide enough information for an automatic decision or     |
| EXCEPTION  | A valid documented exception is active and applicable.                          |

## 7. Audit Evidence

The employee identifier, category, amount, business purpose, approval, decision and exception reference where applicable must be retained for audit purposes.

## 8. Policy Review

This policy must be reviewed annually or when material changes occur to expense approval requirements.
